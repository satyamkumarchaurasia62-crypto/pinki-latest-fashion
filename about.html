How to Create an Account & Log In?

A verified account is mandatory for secure shopping and smooth refunds

Method 1: Via Google Authentication

  1. Click on the Account button at the top of the homepage.
  2. On the login page, click the Google Authentication button and choose your email address.
  3. Once verified, a pop-up will appear—click OK to proceed.
  4. Set your password, security question & answer, verify your mobile number, enter your delivery address, and click Save.

Method 2: Via Manual Sign Up

  1. Click the pink Sign Up button at the bottom of the login page.
  2. Enter your full name, mobile number, email, complete address, and PIN code, then verify your mobile number.
  3. Click Create Account; a confirmation link will be sent to your email address.
  4. After confirming your email, you can log in anytime using your email & password (or via Google).

Important Security Rule: Please enter your exact full name that matches your bank account. Refunds will not be processed to any third-party bank account. Account creation requires both email and mobile verification.

Return, Exchange, Refund & Cancellation Policy

Please read these rules carefully before placing an order

Unboxing Video is Mandatory:

Recording a clear, uncut 360-degree unboxing video while opening the parcel is strictly mandatory. If you receive a torn or damaged item, you must submit a request within 24 to 48 hours of delivery along with the unboxing video and clear photos of the defect. Requests without video proof or after 48 hours will not be accepted.

1. Order Cancellation Rules:

  • Orders can only be cancelled within 24 hours of placement. Once accepted or dispatched, an order cannot be cancelled.
  • For prepaid (Online UPI) orders, you must provide your valid UPI ID or Bank Account details along with the payment screenshot while cancelling to process the refund.

2. Return & Exchange Rules:

  • Returns are accepted only in two cases: (a) Size fitting issues or (b) Product dislike.
  • The outfit must be returned in its original, unused condition with all tags intact. Washed, stained, or customer-damaged items will be rejected.
  • Return Pickup Charges: If there is no fault from our side (i.e., no damage or wrong item) and you return an item due to size or personal preference, return courier pickup charges must be borne by the customer (deducted from the refund amount). If the mistake is ours, return pickup is 100% free.
  • For size issues, you can also opt for an Exchange for another size or outfit instead of a return.

3. Refund Process:

  • Once your return request is approved, the pickup courier ID will be updated in your order section.
  • After the returned parcel reaches our warehouse and passes quality inspection, your refund will be credited directly to your UPI ID or Bank Account within 12 to 27 hours.
